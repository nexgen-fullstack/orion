FAQ

Short answers to the most common questions.

What is ORION?

ORION (ORION Web Studio) is a web design and development studio in Funchal, Madeira, Portugal. We design and build websites, online stores, web apps and AWS cloud systems for businesses in Madeira and worldwide. The studio has 13 years of experience and is led by an AWS Certified Solutions Architect. The name comes from the Orion constellation — visible from every corner of the Earth, just like the brands we help to shine.

Do you only work with businesses in Madeira?

No. We work remotely with clients across Portugal, Europe and worldwide. In Madeira we can also meet in person.

How long does it take to build a website?

A business website or landing page usually takes 1–3 weeks from the first call to launch. Web apps and online stores are planned individually.

How much does a website cost?

Websites start from €199 — a launch price for new clients. The exact fixed price is agreed after a short call, before work starts — no hidden costs. Online stores, web apps and cloud projects are quoted individually.

What is GEO and why does my business need it?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) prepares your site so AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity understand and recommend your business. We add structured data, clear answers and the technical signals AI search relies on.

Can my website be in several languages?

Yes. We build multilingual websites in English, Portuguese, German, Spanish, Italian, French, Ukrainian and other languages. Each language gets its own page with correct hreflang tags, so Google and AI assistants show every visitor the right version.

Can you take care of hosting, domain and updates?

Yes. Domain, hosting, SSL, backups, analytics and ongoing updates can all be handled for you, so you can focus on your business.
